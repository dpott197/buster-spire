    Controls

    ←→ or AD
    Run
    ↑↓ or WS
    Aim up or down. Down drops through grates
    Z J Space
    Jump. Press again in mid-air to double-jump, or against a wall to wall-jump
    X K
    Tap to fire (up to three shots on screen). Hold, then release for a charged shot
    C L Shift
    Slide along the ground, under shots. In mid-air, dash sideways
    Esc P
    Pause (online, only the host can pause)
    Controller
    Stick or d-pad to move and aim, A jump, X or Y shoot (hold to charge), B or any bumper or trigger slide, Start to begin or pause

    Every robot has three energy. A small shot takes one, a half charge takes two, and a full charge or a stomp takes all three. Robots flash for a moment after a hit and can't be hurt again. Your own shots can't hurt you. A round ends after 90 seconds if no one wins it. Online, the host's browser runs the match, so the host should keep this page open.

    Weapon capsules

    Capsules drop into the arena during each round. Touch one to open it. Special shots fire before your buster and don't charge. Everything resets at the end of the round.

    Bomb shot
    Three lobbed bombs that explode on impact
    Ricochet laser
    Three fast beams that bounce off walls
    Shock field
    Two shots that leave a crackling field rivals can't touch
    Drill shot
    Three shots that bore through one block
    Barrier
    Blocks the next hit completely
    Jet boots
